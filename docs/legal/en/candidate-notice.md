**The tool we use and automated processing.** We use Nyra, recruitment software. Nyra automatically extracts text and data from CVs (for example skills and experience) and may order candidates by how well they match the job requirements, based on the keywords in the posting and the similarity between the CV text and the job description. **No rejection or selection decision is taken automatically.** Every application is reviewed by a person on our team, who can disregard the suggested order. You may request human intervention, express your view and contest a decision. You may ask for an explanation of the role of the tool in assessing you.

**Who we share your data with.** Our recruitment team and the Nyra provider (TETA ABC SRL), acting on our behalf. Nyra in turn uses: Supabase (storage, Ireland), Vercel (hosting), Clerk (recruiter authentication), LlamaIndex (extracting data from CVs) and Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (processing service, Netherlands). Current list: nyra.joben.eu/subprocessors. Some of them are in the USA; transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or on standard contractual clauses.

Write to {{COMPANY EMAIL}}. We reply within one month. You have the right to lodge a complaint with ANSPDCP (dataprotection.ro).

**Is it mandatory to give us the data?** Name and email are necessary for us to be able to assess your application. The rest of the data is at your discretion.
