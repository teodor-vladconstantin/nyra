## 1. What this document is about

This policy explains what personal data Nyra processes about **you as a recruiter or application user, and about people who write to us from the website**. For this data, TETA ABC SRL is the **controller**.

The data of **candidates** that Customers upload to Nyra is processed by us as a **processor**, on the Customer's behalf. For that, see the Data Processing Agreement (DPA). A candidate who wants to exercise their rights should first contact the company they applied to; we help that company respond.

TETA ABC SRL has not appointed a data protection officer (DPO). The data protection contact point is privacy@joben.eu. [TO BE VERIFIED BY LAWYER: whether appointing a DPO is mandatory.]

## 2. What data we process, why and on what basis

| Category | Data | Purpose | Basis (GDPR) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Account | Email address, authentication data (password as a hash, verification codes), user identifier | Creating and securing the account, authentication | Art. 6(1)(b), performance of a contract |
| Organisation and access | Organisation name, role (Administrator or Viewer), creation date | Separating data between organisations and access rights | Art. 6(1)(b) |
| Activity in the application | Your identifier as author of notes and scorecards, saved filters, product tour state | Operating the application | Art. 6(1)(b) |
| Activity log | User identifier, action, object type, time (without candidates' personal content) | Security, traceability, defending rights | Art. 6(1)(f), legitimate interest |
| Registration security | Anti-abuse signals (Cloudflare Turnstile, Clerk Protect) | Preventing fake accounts | Art. 6(1)(f) |
| Technical data | IP address, browser type, time and route of the request, in logs | Operation, security, troubleshooting | Art. 6(1)(f) |
| Website forms | Name, email, message (contact form, waiting list) | To reply or tell you when plans are ready | Art. 6(1)(f), legitimate interest; for the waiting list, Art. 6(1)(a), consent |
| Statistics and errors | Planned, not active yet (PostHog, Sentry) | Improving the product, fixing errors | Art. 6(1)(a) for statistics (consent); Art. 6(1)(f) for errors |

We do not take automated decisions with legal effects about you as a user.

You are not legally obliged to give us account data, but without it we cannot create your account.

## 3. Where the data comes from

From you directly (account, forms), from the application (activity) and from Clerk (authentication). Technical data comes automatically from the browser.

## 4. Who we share it with (recipients)

We share data only with necessary suppliers (subprocessors) and with authorities where the law requires it. The current list, with countries and transfer mechanisms, is on the **Subprocessors** page. Currently: Clerk (authentication), Supabase (database, EU region), Vercel (application hosting), Cloudflare (anti-abuse check at registration) and, for candidate data, LlamaIndex and Oracle Cloud Infrastructure. We do not sell data and do not use it for advertising.

## 8. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection (for processing based on legitimate interest), plus the right to withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing. Write to privacy@joben.eu; we reply within one month. Account deletion is done on request by email until a button is implemented in the application. [TO BE COMPLETED]

## 9. Security

We apply technical and organisational measures: encrypted transmission (HTTPS with HSTS), separation of data by organisation at database level, private files with temporary links, access roles, an activity log. No measure removes the risk entirely. An incident affecting you is communicated to you as required by law.

## 10. Changes

If we change the policy, we publish the new version with its effective date and notify you in the application of important changes.
