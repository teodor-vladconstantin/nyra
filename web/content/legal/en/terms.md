7.1. The assistive features use tools that extract text and data from CVs (through LlamaCloud, see the Subprocessors page), normalisation rules and a matching calculation. 7.2. Results are indicative. They may be wrong or incomplete, for instance if a CV has an unusual format or a skill is phrased differently. The Service flags some CVs as needing review.

7.3. **Nyra does not reject, select or hire candidates.** No status change happens automatically. The final decision always belongs to a person in the Customer's organisation, who must actually review the results and be able to disregard them.
